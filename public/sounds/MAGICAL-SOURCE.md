# Magical welcome – genuine xylophone with gentle glockenspiel

File: `magical-welcome.wav`. It preserves the four-note real xylophone
recording from `xylophone-welcome.wav` and adds two quiet, delayed
real glockenspiel notes (C6 and G6). The final tail fades smoothly.

Samples:
- `Percussion/Glock/glock_medium_C6.wav`
- `Percussion/Glock/glock_medium_G6.wav`

Source: [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE),
commit `440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e`.

The original xylophone provenance is described in `XYLOPHONE-SOURCE.md`.
The mixed sound is a local WAV asset; the app makes no external network
requests when playing it.
