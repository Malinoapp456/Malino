# Acoustic xylophone welcome — provenance and license

File: `xylophone-welcome.wav` (short four-note welcome, assembled from
actual xylophone samples, not an oscillator).

Original recording library: [VSCO 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), source commit
`440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e`.

Source samples in `Percussion/Xylo/`:

The final jingle was trimmed, sequenced, gently faded, mixed and exported
as 24 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV. Only the edited WAV is shipped in the app;
no third-party network request is needed during playback.
